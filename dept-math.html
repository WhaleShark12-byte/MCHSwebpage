MathematicsDepartment

Building logical thinkers and problem-solvers who are prepared for college, career, and life. From foundational algebra to AP Calculus, the Raiders do the math.

"Our goal is not just to teach students how to solve problems — it's to help them think through any challenge they face, in math and in life."

Curriculum

Course Offerings

Core

Pre-Algebra

Foundational number sense, operations, fractions, ratios, and an introduction to variables and algebraic thinking.

Grades 8–91 Credit
Core

Algebra I

Solving equations and inequalities, graphing linear and quadratic functions, and an introduction to polynomials.

Grades 9–101 Credit
Core

Geometry

Properties of shapes, congruence, similarity, coordinate geometry, proofs, transformations, and measurement.

Grade 101 Credit
Core

Algebra II

Advanced equations, complex numbers, exponential and logarithmic functions, sequences, and series.

Grade 111 Credit
Advanced

Pre-Calculus

Trigonometry, analytic geometry, conic sections, vectors, polar coordinates, and limits as a foundation for Calculus.

Grades 11–121 Credit
AP Course

AP Calculus AB

Differential and integral calculus including limits, derivatives, applications of derivatives, and definite integrals.

Grade 121 Credit
Advanced

Statistics

Data collection, analysis, probability, distributions, hypothesis testing, and interpreting statistical results.

Grades 11–121 Credit
Elective

Financial Mathematics

Personal budgeting, interest, loans, investing, taxes, insurance, and real-world financial decision-making.

Grades 10–120.5 Credit
Elective

Mathematical Reasoning

Critical thinking, logic, problem-solving strategies, discrete mathematics, and applications to everyday decisions.

Grades 10–120.5 Credit
